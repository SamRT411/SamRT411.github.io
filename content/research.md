My research is concerned with mathematical modeling and machine learning methods for epidemiological applications.

## Current directions (example)

### Mathematical Modeling

I develop mathematical models to analyze the spread of spatiotemporal infectious disease. I have calibrated the models to county-level COVID-19 data.

### Machine Learning Methods

I develop clustering methods to locate statistically significant areas with higher incidence of infectious diease.
